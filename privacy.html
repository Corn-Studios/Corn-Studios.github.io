Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 25, 2026

Overview

Corn Studios is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Our desktop applications — including Win11 Optimizer, CornDownloader, CornTools, CornWatch, and any related tools — do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data whatsoever.

What we collect

Contact form data only.

When you submit a message through the contact form on our website, we collect the following information:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The contents of your message

We do not use cookies, analytics, tracking scripts, or any other data collection mechanisms on our website.

How we use it

Only to respond to you.

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your inquiry. We will never sell, share, rent, or disclose your information to any third party for any reason.

Contact form submissions are processed and stored by Formspree, our form handling provider. You can review Formspree's privacy policy on their website.

Retention

Only as long as needed.

We retain contact form submissions only as long as needed to respond to and resolve your inquiry. After that, messages may be deleted at our discretion.

Your rights

You can request deletion.

You have the right to request that we delete any personal information you have submitted to us. To make a request, email us at the address below and we will process it promptly.
